TABULAE

Privacy Policy — Effective MMXXVI

1. Who We Are

TABULAE is a curating and auction-style resale shop of archived and vintage garments, operated by Ian Avicain ("we", "our"). We operate exclusively through our official Facebook Page — items are posted, bids are received through post comment/chat, and transactions are coordinated via Facebook chat (Messenger).

Contact: aviancm3@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

  • Bidding & orders: your Facebook display name and the bid amounts you post on our auction posts or send via chat.
  • Shipping details: when you win an auction, the recipient name, phone number, and delivery address you provide in Messenger for courier purposes.
  • Payment proof: screenshot or reference number of your payment (via bank transfer, GCash, COD receipt, or the platform you paid through), used only for order confirmation.

We collect no card or bank numbers. We do not host payments ourselves.

3. How Your Information Is Used

  • To process and ship your winning bid to the address you gave.
  • To communicate about bidding status, deadline results, payment, and delivery.
  • To keep accurate sales and expense records for legal/tax purposes.

4. Sharing to Third Parties

Shipping information is shared only with the courier handling the parcel (J&T, Flash, LBC, or partners listed at checkout). No personal data is sold, leased, or used for advertising to outside parties.

5. Bidding Rules Summary

  • Bidding takes place in the comments/chat of each auction post until the posted deadline.
  • After deadline closes, the highest bidder is contacted first. If a bidder matches or commits to the shop's "Best Buy-It-Now" price before deadline, they may lock the item and win it outright.
  • Winning bidders must complete full payment before the agreed delivery date. Unsettled payments by that date forfeit the win — the item is returned to the pool and offered to the second-highest bidder, who will be messaged immediately.
  • Minimum bid increment is ₱50 — each new bid must exceed the current highest by at least ₱50.
  • Bid retractions are not allowed once placed. Repeated retracts, backing out after winning, or fake bids to waste time = automatic and permanent ban from all future auctions, announced publicly on the TABULAE Facebook Page.
  • All transactions, shipping updates, upcoming drops, and promotions are posted on the TABULAE Facebook Page — notifications for our posts should be turned on to catch deadline changes.
  • Shipping is capped at ₱250 for all items. Free shipping applies if the winning total is ₱800 or higher, or if two high-value items are bought with a combined total of ₱800+.

6. No Return Policy

All items are pre-owned, archived, or vintage; each piece is unique and thoroughly inspected before listing. Listings include full transparency — detailed photos, measurements/sizing, condition notes, and authenticity indicators. All questions are entertained before bidding — please ask everything before you commit a bid.

Because bidding is a firm commitment and inventory is singular, all sales are final — no returns or exchanges unless the item received is materially different from its listing (wrong item, damage not disclosed). In that case contact us within 24 hours of delivery with photo evidence and we will work out a remedy.

7. Data Retention

Bid logs, shipping information, and payment confirmations are retained only as long as needed to complete each order and satisfy bookkeeping/legal requirements, then discarded.

8. Your Rights — RA 10173 (Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012)

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or lodge a complaint, by messaging the TABULAE Facebook Page or emailing aviancm3@gmail.com.

9. Payment Methods — No COD for Now

As TABULAE is a fairly new venture, Cash on Delivery (COD) is not yet available. We're still completing the required paperwork and courier arrangements to offer COD through our delivery partners. We expect to add COD once these requirements are settled — for the meantime, available payment options are GCash (primary), Maya, or GoTyme Bank transfer, confirmed via payment proof before shipment.

We appreciate your understanding and patience — trust is built in both directions, and this step keeps every early transaction simple and secure.

10. Cookies & Analytics

This page uses no cookies or trackers. Bidding and communication happen on Facebook's platform, governed by its own policies.
